# 14. Accidents and disasters

Building collapse, fire, crime, and road accidents occur in the city. Damage affects buildings, lives, and living conditions and appears in epoch results.

## 14.1. Collapse and fire

Collapse caused by deterioration or fire can injure or kill residents and workers. Ruins remain and harm surrounding living conditions. See [buildings](05_buildings.md) for maintenance. Fire damage depends on fire resistance and surrounding density. Dense wooden neighborhoods are particularly prone to fire spreading between buildings.

Fires are prevented where firefighting services supplied by fire stations reach the building. Firefighting services cannot travel arbitrarily far; distant fire stations and congestion increase potential damage.

## 14.2. Road accidents

Speed limits unsuitable for road geometry, concentrated traffic, and street parking beyond dedicated spaces increase accident risk. Accidents can cause casualties and reduce the city's evaluation ([paths and nodes](03_paths-and-nodes.md), [scores and achievements](16_scores-and-achievements.md)).

## 14.3. Crime and public safety

Warning signs of crime arise from resident and visiting citizens' `potential_criminality` and local public safety, and can develop into incidents. Policing services supplied by police stations can prevent that progression. Police capacity and service reach both matter. Public safety also affects residents' lives and housing choices ([environment](13_environment.md)).
