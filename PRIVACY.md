# City Genesis Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy explains what information <Operator name> ("the developer") receives in connection with the game "City Genesis" ("the game") and how that information is handled.

## Information the developer receives

Each time the Steam version of the game starts, it communicates with the developer's authentication server to confirm that you own the game on Steam. The developer receives the following information:

- The ID of the Steam account you are playing on (SteamID). If you are borrowing the game through Family Sharing, the ID of the lending account is also received.
- The list of the developer's Steam apps that the account owns (the game, the Playtest, and DLC)
- The IP address the request came from and the time of the request

Authentication uses a temporary ticket issued by Steam. The ticket is used only for the check and is not stored.

The game does not ask you to create an account and does not receive your name, email address, postal address, or payment information. Steam handles purchases and payments. The game shows no advertising and has no feature that sends analytics or crash reports to the developer.

## Purposes of use

The information is used only for the following purposes:

- To confirm that you own the game and its DLC, and to make the DLC you own available
- To prevent unauthorized use and to investigate authentication failures

It is not used for advertising or marketing, and it is not sold.

## Retention and storage

Records on the authentication server are deleted automatically after 30 days.

The authentication result (including your SteamID and the list of owned apps) is also stored on your PC, so that you can play as authenticated for 30 days after your last authentication even without an internet connection. This record is encrypted with a Windows feature and cannot be read from another PC or by another Windows user.

## External services

- **Google Cloud** (Google LLC): Used to operate the authentication server. The server is located in the Tokyo region, but records may be stored on servers outside Japan, such as in the United States. See [Google Cloud Privacy](https://cloud.google.com/privacy) for details.
- **Steam** (Valve Corporation): Purchase and delivery of the game, and Steam features such as the overlay and the Workshop, are handled under the [Steam Privacy Policy](https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/).

Except as required by law, the developer does not share the information it receives with third parties.

## Publishing to the Workshop

When you publish a scenario to the Steam Workshop, the scenario file, title, description, images, and the author name you entered are published on the Workshop, linked to your Steam account. You can change the visibility of or delete published items from the Steam Workshop pages.

## Data stored on your PC

Save data, your own scenarios, settings, and operation logs are stored only on your PC and are not sent to the developer. Publishing to the Workshop happens only when you choose to do so.

## Residents of the EEA and the United Kingdom

The information above is processed to provide the game you purchased (performance of a contract) and to prevent unauthorized use (legitimate interests). You have the right to request access to, rectification, erasure, and restriction of processing of your data, the right to object to processing, and the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority in your country.

To request disclosure, correction, deletion, or suspension of use of your information, contact the address below. To confirm your identity, we will ask for your SteamID.
